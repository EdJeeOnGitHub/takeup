\section*{Preliminary policy-cost and consolidation sensitivity}

This note is a review artifact and is not included in the paper. It uses the
component-wise posterior median of all 1,600 retained draws from the baseline
structural model. It therefore isolates policy assumptions but does not yet
propagate posterior or cluster uncertainty. The latter should use the preferred
exponential cluster-weighted likelihood modes once the server is available.

We minimize the number or illustrative resource cost of distribution sites
while preserving predicted take-up under the experimental allocation. The
population-weighted specifications use census adult population for all 144
communities. Costs include a fixed site cost, the documented \$0.20 Bracelet
cost per expected participant, and a grid for household round-trip travel. The
travel grid is illustrative rather than an estimated welfare parameter.

Population weighting leaves the 3.5 km site-count comparison similar to the
legacy calculation: Bracelet observability requires 78 sites versus 104 under
Control observability, a saving of 26 sites. Restricting assignments to 2.5 km,
the upper end of experimental distance support, is much more consequential:
both regimes require 121 sites. The extensive-margin consolidation result is
therefore driven by allowing assignments between 2.5 and 3.5 km.

At 3.5 km, adding the Bracelet's unit cost reverses the cost comparison under
an illustrative \$100 fixed cost per site. Bracelet saves 26 sites but costs
about \$3,928 more in total; for these allocations, the fixed cost would need
to be about \$251 per site to offset the signal expense. Valuing round-trip
travel at \$0.10 per participant-kilometre raises this local break-even value
to about \$471. At \$0.25, the optimizer opens substantially more sites to
shorten travel, so the site-saving interpretation no longer applies. These are
scenario results, not estimates of welfare.

For the consolidation-information check, every experimental PoT is matched to
its nearest candidate site (all 144 matches are unique). Assignments to any
other site receive a Bracelet observability schedule moved toward Control.
Moving halfway toward Control reduces the site saving from 26 to 13; replacing
Bracelet observability by Control observability on those assignments reduces it
to 4. Thus, the geometric result survives weakly under the most conservative
case but is highly sensitive to assuming that observability is invariant under
site consolidation.

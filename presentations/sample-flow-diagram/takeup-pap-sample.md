# **Sample Construction for Community Mass Deworming Study**

## **Executive Summary**

This study is a cluster-randomized controlled trial conducted in Western Kenya. It evaluates the impact of **social signaling** (via indelible ink and silicon bracelets) and **private incentives** (via calendars) on adult deworming take-up. The intervention explores how observability and social approval can overcome barriers like present bias and the "public good" nature of deworming.

## ---

**Step-by-Step Sample Construction**

### **1\. Cluster Selection and Randomization**

* **Geographic Scope**: The study was implemented in 18 subcounties across Busia, Kakamega, and Siaya counties in Western Kenya.

* **Initial Identification**: Researchers used geospatial data of primary schools to identify 1,451 potential clusters within 2.5 km radii.

* **Final Cluster Count**: After excluding clusters where implementation was infeasible, the study was finalized with **144 clusters**.

* **Randomization**: These 144 clusters were randomly assigned to one of four incentive arms: Control, Ink, Calendar, or Bracelet.

### **2\. Community Census (The Sampling Frame)**

* **Targeting**: A full census was conducted within every targeted village in the 144 clusters prior to the intervention.

* **Eligibility**: Information was collected for all adults aged **15 years or older**.

* **Purpose**: This census served as the master list for selecting individuals for all subsequent surveys and SMS monitoring.

### **3\. Baseline Household Survey**

* **Household Selection**: A random sample of **15 households** was selected from each of the 144 clusters.

* **Participant Selection**: From each selected household, **one adult** eligible for deworming was randomly picked.

* **Expected Sample Size**: Approximately **2,160 individuals** ($144 \\text{ clusters} \\times 15 \\text{ adults}$).

### **4\. Individual SMS Intervention Samples**

The researchers selected specific groups of phone owners to receive text message treatments:

* **In Control Clusters**:  
  * **15 individuals** per cluster were selected for the "Reminder SMS" group.

  * **15 individuals** per cluster were selected for the "Reminder \+ Peer Information SMS" group.

* **In Incentive Clusters (Ink, Calendar, Bracelet)**:  
  * **25 individuals** per cluster were selected to receive the "Reminder \+ Peer Information SMS".

* **SMS Airtime Reward Sub-sample**: In the second wave, **2 individuals per cluster** were randomly picked from each SMS group to test message receipt via a 50 KSh reward.

### **5\. Endline Household Survey**

A new random sample was drawn from each cluster after the intervention to verify take-up and behavioral changes:

* **Total Endline Sample Size**: **25 individuals** per cluster, totaling **3,600 individuals**.

* **Stratification in Control Clusters**:  
  * **15 individuals** who received no SMS.

  * **5 individuals** from the "Reminder SMS" group.

  * **5 individuals** from the "Reminder \+ Peer Info SMS" group.

* **Stratification in Incentive Clusters**:  
  * **20 individuals** who received no SMS.

  * **5 individuals** from the "Reminder \+ Peer Info SMS" group.
